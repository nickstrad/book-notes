## Coordinator decisions (one line each, spec lines in the fix commit)

1. **U19.** A path no route serves is 404 and a method a route does not accept is 405, both in the
   documented error form (`not_found`, `method_not_allowed`); 405 keeps its `Allow` header.
2. **U20 (Q on retry history).** History survives a retry. Attempt `n` numbers every execution of the
   job and is never reused; the lease's `attempt` and the job's `attempts` keep counting toward
   `max_attempts` since the last retry, as the spec's "resets its attempt count" says.
3. **U21.** `delay_seconds` on enqueue and nack is 0–2 147 483 647; a negative value is 400. This
   reverses reconciliation 03's "negative stays accepted as due now", which was recorded as unclaimed;
   E04 claimed it with a retry scheduled before its failure.
4. **U22 (Q-E04-3).** No grace period. A lease ends at `lease_expires_at` by the service clock;
   heartbeat, ack and nack from that instant are 409 whether or not the sweep has released the job.
5. **U23 (Q-E04-2).** "Readiness" is the moment a job last became `queued`. Release by lease expiry
   or worker deletion sets `run_at` to the release time, as an operator retry already does.
6. **A1 (Q-E04-1).** Without `backoff.max_seconds`, each computed wait is capped at 3 600 seconds, or
   at `base_seconds` when that is larger (so a long base still means what it says). The plan's
   default; an explicit `max_seconds` is unchanged.

Not decided here: Q-E04-4 (whether "completed" in stats includes dead and cancelled) — the explorer
filed no report, and `throughput_last_hour` counts `succeeded` only as SPEC says "completed jobs";
left open in the backlog. H-E04-08 (FIFO under a backward clock step), H-E04-09 (stats snapshot) and
H-E04-10 (ack atomicity) stay open hypotheses for later charters.
