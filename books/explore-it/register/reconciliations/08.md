## Coordinator decision for A5

A fixed-time schedule (an hour field that is not a wildcard or a `*/n` step) runs once per
matching local civil time. A matching local time skipped by a spring-forward transition
runs once at the first instant after the gap; a matching local time that occurs twice
runs only at its first occurrence. Wildcard-hour schedules keep following real elapsed
time (an hourly schedule still runs once per real hour across both transitions). This is
the classic cron convention the report names as its comparable oracle; the robfig
library's "skipped interval is not run" behaviour is the one rejected. Existing D23
behaviour (09:00 and 02:00 follow the offset change outside the transition hour) holds.
