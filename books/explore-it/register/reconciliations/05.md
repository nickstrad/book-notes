## Coordinator decisions (one line each, spec lines in the fix commit)

1. **U24 (Q-E05-1).** A release by worker deregistration counts as an unsuccessful attempt, exactly
   like a lease expiry: attempt row `released` / `worker deleted`, and `dead` at `max_attempts`.
2. **U25 (Q-E05-2).** Only a `leased` job has a lease token, holder and expiry; every exit clears all
   three. "Who ran it" lives in the attempt rows (`worker_id`), not on the job.
3. **U26.** An event is labelled with the new state, except a release by the expiry sweep, which is
   `lease_expired`; the label never depends on earlier error text.
4. **A2.** A job leased when its queue is purged keeps its lease: ack completes it; a nack, an expiry
   or a worker deletion makes it `dead`, never pending again. Operator retry clears the mark.

Not decided here: the second half of E05-F02, that a delivery cancelled in flight has no attempt
row. That is part of the open cancel-while-running silence (A3), left for its experiment.
H-E05-21 (a worker that deregisters on SIGTERM; now bounded by U24's fix) and T-E05-1 (an "offset
and keep ticking" clock mode) stay in the backlog.
